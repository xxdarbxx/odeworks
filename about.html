Our Story

Ode Works Motorcycle Dealership

Ode Works started as a small repair shop in Galas, Quezon City in 2007. Today, we've grown into a full-service motorcycle dealership — offering new bikes from six global brands, genuine parts and accessories, and a certified service center trusted by thousands of riders across Metro Manila.

Our mission is simple: make every rider's journey safer, smoother, and more enjoyable — from the moment they walk into our showroom to every service visit after.
