Our Story

Ode Works Repair Shop

Ode Works started as a small repair shop in Galas, Quezon City in 2007. Today, we've grown into a full-service motorcycle repair and maintenance center — trusted by thousands of riders across Metro Manila for honest diagnostics and quality workmanship.

Our mission is simple: fix it right the first time, explain what we're doing and why, and get you back on the road safely.
